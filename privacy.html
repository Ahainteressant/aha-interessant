Privacy Policy – “Aha interessant”

Last updated: 5 September 2026

“Aha interessant” is a private, internal tool that uploads self-produced videos to the operator's own TikTok account. It is not available to third parties.

1. What data is processed

  • The operator's own TikTok authorization tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens), stored securely on the operator's own server and used solely to upload videos to the operator's own account.
  • Video files produced by the operator.

2. What data is NOT processed

The tool collects no personal data of any third party. It has no users other than the operator, sets no cookies, and performs no tracking or analytics. This website itself is a static page without cookies or analytics.

3. Data sharing

Videos are transmitted to TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited) for publication on the operator's own account. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to all data processed by TikTok.

4. Your rights & contact

Since no third-party data is processed, no third party is affected. For any questions or requests under the GDPR, contact: klevtv@web.de

Deutsche Zusammenfassung

Das Werkzeug verarbeitet ausschließlich die eigenen TikTok-Zugriffstoken des Betreibers sowie selbst produzierte Videodateien. Es werden keine personenbezogenen Daten Dritter erhoben, keine Cookies gesetzt und kein Tracking betrieben. Videos werden zur Veröffentlichung an TikTok übertragen; dort gilt die Datenschutzerklärung von TikTok. Verantwortlich und Kontakt: klevtv@web.de
